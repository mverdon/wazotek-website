Frequently Asked Questions

What does a fractional CTO or senior advisor actually do?

We provide senior technical judgment and leadership support for cloud, data, and AI decisions without the cost and overhead of a full-time hire. This includes architecture reviews, vendor evaluation, roadmap planning, team guidance, and decision support for important technology choices.

How is this different from hiring a full-time CTO?

A fractional engagement provides access to senior-level experience without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire. This works well for companies that need strategic technical leadership but don't require someone in the role full-time, or for companies facing major technology decisions that benefit from outside expertise.

How is this different from a software agency or contractor?

WazoTek focuses on advisory, decision support, and technical oversight rather than hands-on delivery or staff augmentation. We help you make better decisions, evaluate options, challenge vendor proposals, and set technical direction. When implementation is required, we help structure the work, select partners, and provide oversight, but execution itself is handled by your team or selected partners.

What kinds of Hong Kong companies are the best fit?

Companies that need senior technical judgment for cloud, data, or AI decisions but don't have that expertise in-house. Typically SMEs, growth-stage companies, or established businesses planning technology modernization. Best fit is leadership teams facing major technology decisions where getting it right matters.

How do engagements usually begin?

Most engagements start with a diagnostic sprint: a fixed-scope assessment focused on a specific challenge such as cloud cost optimization, architecture review, or AI roadmap framing. This provides immediate value and clarity before committing to ongoing work. Many companies then move to a monthly advisory retainer or fractional leadership arrangement.

Are implementation services included?

WazoTek focuses on advisory, decision support, and technical oversight rather than hands-on execution. When implementation is required, we help define requirements, evaluate vendors or contractors, and provide oversight during delivery. Implementation itself is handled by your internal team or selected partners.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is discussed after an initial conversation to ensure fit and scope clarity. Diagnostic sprints and advisory retainers are structured as fixed monthly fees. Fractional leadership engagements are scoped based on required involvement and engagement duration.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

WazoTek is based in Hong Kong and prioritizes work with Hong Kong companies and leadership teams. This allows us to provide responsive in-person support when needed and stay close to the local business context and work in the same time zone as client teams.
