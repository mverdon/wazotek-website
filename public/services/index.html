Services

How engagements work

Fractional CTO and advisory support for Hong Kong companies that need senior judgment without a full-time hire. Most start with a Diagnostic Sprint.

Best First Step

Diagnostic Sprint

A fixed-scope review of architecture, cloud cost, AI opportunities, data platforms, or vendors. You get a written report with clear recommendations.

Typical engagement: 2-3 weeks · fixed fee

Best when you need clarity before committing to ongoing advisory work.

Ongoing Support

Monthly Advisory

Regular input on cloud, data, AI, and architecture decisions. Monthly retainer with scheduled sessions, plus room for urgent questions in between.

For teams that need steady senior judgment without a full-time commitment.

Deep Involvement

Fractional CTO

Deeper involvement in roadmap planning, architecture decisions, team guidance, delivery oversight, and support for executive decisions during transition or scale.

For Hong Kong companies that need fractional CTO leadership in a critical phase, but not a full-time hire. More on what a fractional CTO is.

Ideal fit

Hong Kong SMEs, growth-stage companies, and established firms facing cloud, data, or AI decisions where senior judgment matters, but a full-time CTO is not the right cost or commitment yet.

Not a fit

Hands-on coding staff augmentation, build-from-scratch agency delivery, or very early teams without budget for senior advisory. WazoTek is advisory and oversight. Implementation stays with your team or partners you choose.
