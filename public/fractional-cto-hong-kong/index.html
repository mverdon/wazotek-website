When a Hong Kong company needs a fractional CTO

Usually founders, CEOs, and leadership teams who own the technology outcomes but do not have a permanent CTO on payroll yet (or do not want one). Common moments: growing past the first engineers, cleaning up cloud and data foundations, or weighing AI and major vendors where a bad call is costly.

Still unsure whether you need the role at all? Start with the situations on the homepage. If you already know you need senior judgment part-time, that is what a fractional CTO engagement is for.

Fractional CTO vs agency vs full-time hire

vs a full-time CTO: You get senior judgment without the salary, equity, and overhead of a permanent executive. Useful when you need coverage and decision support, not someone owning the function five days a week.

vs a software agency or contractor: Agencies build. A fractional CTO helps you decide what to build, weigh options, challenge proposals, and oversee delivery. WazoTek does not do staff augmentation or hands-on implementation. Your team or partners still do the build.

What you get: Clearer architecture and roadmap choices, vendor evaluation, team guidance, and a senior technical voice with leadership, sized to the hours and duration you need.

Why being based in Hong Kong matters

WazoTek is based in Hong Kong and focuses on Hong Kong companies and leadership teams. Same time zone for live discussion, local context, and in-person sessions when a whiteboard beats another video call.
